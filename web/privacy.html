Privacy

Last updated 2026-10-25.

This is a personal project run by Daniel Kim. There are no accounts or ads, and nothing here is sold. This page lists what is collected, and by whom.

Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read and how people arrive. It loads only after you press Accept in the cookie banner, and only on the live site. If you decline, no analytics tag loads and no analytics cookies are set. Declining after accepting stops the tag from the next page load; cookies already set stay until they expire. The same Google Analytics property also receives data from danielkimdev.com, my personal site, so reports mix the two.

  • Collected: pages viewed (the full address, including any query string), referrer, approximate location, browser, device and screen size, language, and interaction events such as outbound clicks and scrolls.
  • Cookies: _ga and a second cookie starting with _ga_, which identify a browser anonymously across visits.
  • IP address: Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses; Google receives it with the request that delivers the tag.
  • Processor: Google, under Google's privacy policy. Data may be processed outside your country.
  • Opting out: press Decline, or use a content blocker, your browser's tracking protection, or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Umami

The pages also count visits with Umami Cloud. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data, so it runs without a consent prompt.

The Hall of Fame board

While you play, the game reports your run to a small database so the Hall of Fame on the home page can show it. Each run stores a random run id made in your browser, the name you chose (letters and digits only; ANONYMOUS if you leave it alone), your score and move count, whether the run is finished, the two-letter country code Cloudflare derives from your connection, and start and last-seen times. It does not store your IP address, and it does not show where in the game you are. Finished runs stay on the board; unfinished runs may be deleted after 30 days once the board fills up. You can choose not to be listed when you pick your name. To have a run removed, contact me with the name and approximate date.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare processes requests and keeps technical logs (such as IP address, requested address and browser) to run and secure the service, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Stored in your browser

These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere by this site, except as described above:

  • grues-in-comic/save/v1: your saved game.
  • grues-in-comic/save/v1/cast, /cast/pick: your chosen comic cast and whether you have chosen.
  • grues-in-comic/save/v1/board: your Hall of Fame name and whether you are listed.
  • grues-consent: your cookie choice (accepted or declined).

Clear them any time in your browser's site settings.

Links to other sites

Links to GitHub and other sites are governed by their own policies.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Korea's PIPA) you may be able to ask what is held about you or have it deleted. Analytics data is anonymous and tied only to a browser cookie, so clearing cookies is the practical way to reset it. For anything else, write to contact@danielkimdev.com.
